PRIVACY

Your screen stays yours.

Panda Companion is experimental. Screen sharing is intended to start only after an explicit user action; do not rely on screen capture until the Windows and macOS flows have been tested on supported systems.

Local Ollama

When Local Ollama is selected, prompts, images, and OCR are intended to be sent to the Ollama service on your own computer at 127.0.0.1:11434. Panda does not silently fall back to OpenAI.

OpenAI provider

If you choose OpenAI, questions and any screen context you submit for analysis are sent to OpenAI using your own API key. OpenAI’s handling is governed by your account settings and its current policies.

Stored information

Chat history and app exclusions are stored locally in the app webview. API credentials are stored by the operating system’s secure credential store. Do not share diagnostic logs containing private content.
